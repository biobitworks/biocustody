Biology changes continuously, but audit systems and AI claims are discrete. BioCustody keeps those questions separate. Every observation receives an exact cryptographic identity, while a versioned statistical model determines whether it remains within the same scientific state.

For this demo, we use Cell Painting perturbation profiles. A genetic perturbation moves outside the reference cloud. We rank independently observed compound profiles by how strongly they oppose that perturbation direction. That makes the top result a predicted counter-perturbation candidate, not an experimental rescue claim.

Small molecules can travel as canonical SMILES, while complex peptides remain in BILN or HELM and produce derived molecular representations under the same custody route.

Every source, transformation, tool result, and claim becomes a linked custody object. Change one upstream value and the route diverges, invalidating dependent claims until they are recomputed.

On AWS, an agent can propose the scientific claim, but deterministic custody and policy decide whether it is admissible.
